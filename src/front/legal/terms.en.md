## Computation on your device

The globe is a simulation computed together by its visitors. By opening the page or turning on the wallpaper, you agree that your device performs part of this computation. This uses processor time, battery and a little mobile data. The app lets you compute only while charging, or only while the wallpaper is visible.

## What is not allowed

- Sending false or fabricated results, or passing yourself off as another witness.
- Getting around the connection limits or automating connections in bulk.
- Disrupting the service or other visitors.

Suspicious results are discarded and their author blocked for 24 hours. Your sigil and your contributions are public.
