# Privacy — world.drakar.tech and Android app

Last updated: 17 October 2026

These service-specific terms supplement the [drakar.tech general privacy policy](https://drakar.tech/confidentialite?lang=en). Where they differ, they prevail for this service. For everything else (controller, hosting, rights, contact), the general privacy policy applies.

## What we process

The globe is computed together by its visitors: your browser or your phone does part of the computation. This uses your processor and a little network. You can stop at any time by closing the page or turning off the wallpaper.

- **Sigil**: a random identifier created on your device to recognise your contributions. It contains neither your name nor your address. Failing that, the server derives it from a fingerprint of your IP address and your browser.
- **Contributions**: for each computation, the sigil, the date, the region computed and how long it took. The sigil and the contribution statistics are **public**: they appear in the globe and its chronicle.
- **IP address**: kept in memory to limit abuse. In case of cheating, the IP address and the sigil are blocked for 24 hours and the IP address is recorded in the server log.
- The battery and network status are read on your device to adapt the pace of computation. They are not sent.
- The Android app only asks for access to the Internet and to the network status. It includes no analytics or crash-reporting tool.

Legal basis: our legitimate interest in running a shared computation and in keeping cheating out.

The stock market prices shown come from financial services queried by our server alone: no data about you is passed on to them.

## Storage on your device

| Name | Purpose | Duration |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `drakar.sigil` | Recognise your contributions | Until deleted |
| `drakar.lang` | Remember the chosen language | Until deleted |

In the Android app, the sigil and your settings stay in the app's storage and disappear when it is uninstalled.

## How long

| Data | Duration |
| --- | --- |
| IP address used to limit abuse | For the duration of the connection, in memory |
| Block for cheating | 24 hours |
| Contributions and statistics linked to the sigil | 12 months |
| The globe's chronicle, cities and routes | Kept, but the sigil is removed from them after 12 months |

## Exercising your rights

Include your sigil in your request. It is shown at the top right of the globe and, in the Android app, on its settings screen, under “Witness”. Without it, we cannot find your contributions, because we do not know who you are.
